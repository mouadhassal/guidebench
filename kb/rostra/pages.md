# page: home | Rostra: shift scheduling for hourly teams

## What Rostra does
Rostra is shift scheduling and time tracking software for businesses that run on hourly staff: hotels, restaurants, event staffing agencies and retail chains. Managers build rotas by drag and drop, staff swap shifts in the mobile app, and approved hours flow to payroll as a clean export. Rostra does not run payroll itself; it prepares hours for your payroll or accounting system.

## Who uses Rostra
More than 1,400 businesses in Europe schedule their teams with Rostra. Typical customers have between 20 and 2,000 hourly workers spread across one or many locations.

## Results customers report
Kalev Events, an event staffing agency in Tallinn, cut the time spent building weekly rotas by 38% in its first quarter on Rostra. Nordhavn Hotels reduced unplanned overtime by 11% across 9 hotels.

# page: features | Features

## Scheduling
Drag-and-drop rota builder with templates, open shifts that staff can claim, and shift swaps that a manager approves with one tap. Availability and time-off requests are collected in the app. The auto-scheduler fills a week of shifts from availability, skills and labour-budget rules.

## Time tracking
Staff clock in and out in the mobile app with GPS geofencing, or on a shared tablet kiosk with a 4-digit PIN. The mobile app works offline and syncs when the connection returns. Breaks are tracked automatically against your break rules.

## Labour cost control
Rostra shows the planned labour cost of a rota against your sales forecast and warns when a shift would trigger overtime. Overtime rules can be set per country and per contract type.

## Mobile apps
Native apps for iOS and Android. Staff get push notifications for new shifts, swaps and schedule changes.

## Reporting
Hours, overtime, absence and labour-cost reports, exportable to CSV or Excel. Growth and Enterprise plans include scheduled reports by email.

# page: integrations | Integrations

## Payroll and accounting
Rostra exports approved hours to Xero, QuickBooks Online and Merit Aktiva. Payroll exports are available on the Growth and Enterprise plans.

## HR systems
Two-way sync with Personio for employee records and absences.

## Calendars and messaging
Shifts can be subscribed to in Google Calendar. Managers can receive approval requests in Slack.

## API and webhooks
A REST API is available on the Growth and Enterprise plans, with a rate limit of 600 requests per minute. Webhooks notify your systems about published rotas and approved timesheets.

# page: faq | Frequently asked questions

## Can Rostra run our payroll?
No. Rostra prepares approved hours and exports them to your payroll or accounting system. Payroll itself stays in your existing system.

## Does Rostra work for office teams?
Rostra is built for shift-based work. Salaried office teams with fixed hours usually do not need it.

## Can we import existing schedules?
Yes, schedules and employee lists can be imported from CSV or Excel files.

## How long does it take to go live?
Most Growth customers publish their first rota within 2 weeks of signing up.
