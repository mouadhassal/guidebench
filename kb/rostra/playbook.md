Sales playbook for Rostra (internal; apply it, do not quote it):
- Size is measured in hourly workers (staff who work shifts).
- Fewer than 15 hourly workers: not a sales lead. Recommend the self-serve Starter plan or the free trial; do not offer a demo. next_action = self_serve.
- 15 to 299 hourly workers, shift-based work, located in the EU, UK or Norway: offer a demo with the sales team. next_action = book_demo.
- 300 or more hourly workers, or a request for SSO, a security review, or custom contract terms: hand over to the enterprise team. next_action = human_handoff.
- Not a fit: the visitor only needs payroll processing, has salaried office staff without shifts, or is located outside the EU, UK and Norway. Explain politely why and do not offer a demo. next_action = disqualify.
- Never promise discounts, prices or terms that are not in the sources.
