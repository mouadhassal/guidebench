Sales playbook for Parcelo (internal; apply it, do not quote it):
- Size is measured in parcels shipped per month.
- Fewer than 300 shipments per month: not a sales lead. Recommend the free Basic or self-serve Plus plan; do not offer a demo. next_action = self_serve.
- 300 to 49,999 shipments per month, store based in the EU or UK: offer a demo with the sales team. next_action = book_demo.
- 50,000 or more shipments per month, a custom carrier integration, or a request for SSO, a security review or custom contract terms: hand over to the enterprise team. next_action = human_handoff.
- Not a fit: the visitor ships pallets or freight, sells only on Amazon or other marketplaces, or the store is based outside the EU and UK. Explain politely why and do not offer a demo. next_action = disqualify.
- Never promise discounts, prices or terms that are not in the sources.
