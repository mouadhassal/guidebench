# page: home | Parcelo: delivery updates your shoppers trust

## What Parcelo does
Parcelo gives online stores a branded tracking page and automatic delivery notifications for every parcel, across carriers. Shoppers stop asking "where is my order", and support teams get fewer tickets. Parcelo is built for parcels sent to consumers; it does not handle pallets, freight or warehouse management.

## Who uses Parcelo
Over 2,600 online stores use Parcelo, from small Shopify shops to brands shipping tens of thousands of parcels per month.

## Results customers report
Nordic Knit cut "where is my order" support tickets by 46% in two months. Vello Bikes raised repeat purchases from its tracking page by 8%.

# page: features | Features

## Tracking page
A branded tracking page on your own domain, available in 32 languages, with product recommendations and your logo and colours.

## Notifications
Automatic email notifications at each delivery step. SMS notifications are available as an add-on at €0.06 per message.

## Delay prediction
Parcelo predicts late deliveries from carrier scan patterns and alerts your team before the shopper notices.

## Returns portal
A self-service returns portal where shoppers register returns and print labels. The returns portal is included on the Scale and Enterprise plans.

## Carrier coverage
Parcelo tracks shipments from more than 1,100 carriers, including DPD, Omniva, Itella SmartPost, DHL, UPS, GLS and Venipak.

# page: pricing | Pricing

## Plans
Basic is free for up to 50 shipments per month. Plus costs €39 per month for up to 1,000 shipments per month. Scale costs €129 per month and includes 4,000 shipments per month, with each extra shipment at €0.03. Enterprise pricing is custom and starts from 50,000 shipments per month.

## Annual billing
Annual billing on Plus and Scale gives a 20% discount. There are no other standard discounts; special terms are only agreed by the sales team.

## Free trial
Plus and Scale can be tried free for 30 days.

# page: integrations | Integrations

## Store platforms
Parcelo connects to Shopify, WooCommerce and Adobe Commerce (Magento). Orders sync automatically within minutes.

## Marketing and support tools
Delivery events can trigger flows in Klaviyo. Parcel status appears inside tickets in Zendesk and Gorgias.

## Not supported today
Parcelo has no integration with Amazon or other marketplaces, because marketplaces control their own delivery messages.

## API
Scale and Enterprise include a REST API and webhooks for delivery events.

# page: faq | Frequently asked questions

## Do we need to change carriers?
No. Parcelo works with the carriers you already use.

## Can Parcelo print shipping labels?
Only for returns, through the returns portal. Outbound labels stay in your shipping software.

## How fast is setup?
Most Shopify stores publish their tracking page within 1 day.

## Does Parcelo work for B2B freight?
No. Parcelo is built for parcels to consumers, not pallets or freight.
