# page: home | Invoxa: purchase invoices on autopilot

## What Invoxa does
Invoxa automates accounts payable: it captures incoming purchase invoices from email, PDF, scans and e-invoice networks, routes them through approval workflows, matches them to purchase orders and posts them to your accounting system. Invoxa handles incoming (purchase) invoices only; it does not create or send sales invoices to your customers.

## Who uses Invoxa
Finance teams at companies that receive from about 50 to several thousand purchase invoices per month. Over 900 companies in the Nordics and Baltics use Invoxa.

## Results customers report
Saar Logistics reduced the average time from invoice receipt to approval from 9 days to 2 days. Pinewood Retail Group processes 3,200 invoices per month with a finance team of 3 people.

# page: features | Features

## Invoice capture
Invoxa reads header fields and line items from PDFs, scans and photos. Capture accuracy for header fields was 97.2% on our 2025 benchmark of real customer invoices. Invoices can arrive in 24 languages.

## Approval workflows
Rules route each invoice to the right approver by supplier, cost centre, amount or project. Approvers can approve from email or the mobile app. Substitutes are assigned automatically during holidays.

## Purchase-order matching
Two-way and three-way matching against purchase orders and goods receipts. Matching is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans.

## E-invoicing
Invoxa is a certified Peppol access point and supports the Estonian e-invoice standard, so e-invoices arrive without manual entry.

## Archive
All invoices and approval trails are archived for 7 years to meet bookkeeping rules, and can be searched by any field.

# page: integrations | Integrations

## Accounting and ERP systems
Invoxa posts approved invoices to Merit Aktiva, Directo, Xero, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and SAP Business One.

## Not supported today
Invoxa does not integrate with QuickBooks or with SAP S/4HANA. Customers on other or custom ERP systems are handled by the enterprise team.

## Identity
Single sign-on with Microsoft Entra ID is available on Pro. SAML single sign-on with any identity provider is available on Enterprise.

## API
Pro and Enterprise include a REST API and webhooks for invoice events.

# page: faq | Frequently asked questions

## Can Invoxa send invoices to our customers?
No. Invoxa only processes incoming purchase invoices. For sales invoicing, use your accounting system.

## Can Invoxa pay our suppliers?
Invoxa creates payment files for approved invoices that you upload to your bank. It does not move money itself.

## How long does onboarding take on Pro?
Most Pro customers are live within 3 weeks.

## Can we keep our existing approval rules?
Yes. Approval rules are configured during onboarding to match your current process.
